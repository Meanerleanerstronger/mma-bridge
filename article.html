Islam vs Arman 2 Official

By MMA Bridge  ·  December 2025

The UFC has officially announced the rematch between lightweight champion Islam Makhachev and rising contender Arman Tsarukyan. Their first meeting showcased elite grappling exchanges and tactical striking that laid the foundation for one of the most anticipated rematches in the division.

Since their first bout, both fighters have evolved. Makhachev has solidified his dominance with championship performances, while Tsarukyan has sharpened his striking and defensive wrestling, positioning himself as a legitimate threat to the throne.

With stylistic adjustments on both sides, this rematch promises higher stakes, deeper strategy, and a clearer answer to who truly rules the lightweight division.
