About

MMA Bridge

Built by a fan, for fans who actually watch the fights.

Every other option was built for someone else. Tapology is a great stats database — deep and useful, but clinical. Built for researchers, not fans. ESPN is a broadcast network that treats you like a casual viewer. Neither gives you a real place to pick fights, compete on it, argue rankings, and actually feel something about the sport.

There was nothing built for the fan who watches every card, has strong opinions on every matchup, and wants to back those opinions up. So this got built.

What's here

Pick fight winners before every card locks — winner, method, round. Climb from Rookie to Legend based on pure accuracy. Run a private pick'em league with your crew all season — standings, head-to-head, the whole thing. Check P4P rankings and cast your vote. Read and write event reviews after fight night. And ask Lucas — our MMA AI — anything about any fighter, any event, any result. That's MMA Bridge.

Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 2026
What We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used only for login and account recovery
  • Display name — the name shown on leaderboards and your profile
  • Fight picks — your predictions for UFC events
  • Hype ratings — your 1–10 event ratings
  • Favourite fighters — fighters you follow for notifications
  • Group membership — if you join or create a private pick'em group

We do not collect payment information, phone numbers, or any other personal data.

How We Use It
  • Running the pick'em game and tracking your accuracy over the season
  • Showing your stats on leaderboards and your public profile
  • Sending fight-night notifications if you enable push alerts
  • Letting you compete in private groups with friends

We do not sell your data, run ads, or share your information with any third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

MMA Bridge uses Supabase for authentication and database storage. Your email and account data are stored on Supabase's infrastructure. See Supabase's privacy policy for details on how they handle data.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visit stats may be collected by GitHub's infrastructure per their privacy policy.

Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser localStorage to store:

  • Your login session token (Supabase auth)
  • Notification preferences and seen-notification IDs
  • Your theme preference (dark/light mode)
  • Temporary pick state to prevent data loss on refresh

No advertising cookies or cross-site trackers are used.

Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time.

  • Delete your account — go to your Profile page, scroll to the Danger Zone, and click "Delete My Account." This permanently removes your profile, all picks, all ratings, and signs you out. It cannot be undone.
  • Data export or correction requests — email contact@mmabridge.com and we'll respond within 30 days.
  • EU/UK users (GDPR) — you have the right to object to processing, request portability, or lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
Data Retention

Your data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data is permanently removed within 24 hours. We do not retain backups of deleted accounts beyond our standard infrastructure backup window (7 days).

Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Authentication is handled by Supabase with industry-standard session management. Passwords are never stored by MMA Bridge — login is handled entirely by Supabase Auth.

Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
