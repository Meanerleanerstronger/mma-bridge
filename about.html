How It Works

Your Guide to MMA Bridge

MMA Bridge is a year-long UFC pick'em league. You and your friends compete every event — pick winners, earn points, climb the leaderboard, and see who really knows their MMA by the end of the season.

Step 1
🔑 Sign Up & Join a Group
Everything is free. Create an account, then either create your own group or get an invite link from a friend and join theirs. Your group is your private league — you only compete against the people in it.
01
Go to Sign Up — create an account with your email.
02
Head to the Leaderboard page and click Create Group. Give it a name — this makes you the commissioner.
03
Share your group's invite link with friends. They click it and get added automatically — no manual approval needed.
04
As commissioner you can also set a season start date to zero everyone's stats and kick off a fresh competition.
Step 2
🥊 Make Your Fight Picks
Before each event starts, go to Upcoming Events, click any card, and hit Pick Fights. Choose the winner for as many bouts as you want — main card, prelims, early prelims. The more picks you make, the more data counts towards your accuracy score.
01
Picks lock when the event starts. A countdown on each card shows you exactly how long you have. Once locked, no changes.
02
You can see the Community % on each fight — how the whole site is leaning. Don't just follow the crowd; going against the grain and being right earns you the Upset King badge.
03
You'll also see X/Y in group on each fighter — that's how many of your group members picked them. Know who's fading who before the card goes live.
04
After the event, results are entered and your picks are marked correct ✓ or wrong ✗ automatically.
Step 3
📊 How Scoring Works
Your score is accuracy — the percentage of your picks that were correct, across all judged fights. It's not about volume; picking 5 fights and getting 4 right (80%) beats picking 20 and going 12/20 (60%). You need at least 10 judged picks to move off the entry tier.
✅
Correct Pick
You picked the winner. Counts toward your accuracy score.
⏳
Pending
Fight hasn't happened yet, or results haven't been entered.
❌
Wrong Pick
You picked the loser. Still counts toward your total — no hiding.
🏆
Accuracy %
Correct ÷ Total judged picks. This is your rank on the leaderboard.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions
When do picks lock?
Picks lock the moment the event's main card starts. A live countdown on each event card tells you exactly how long you have. Once locked, you can't change or add picks for that event.
What if I only pick the main card and skip prelims?
That's fine. Your accuracy is calculated only from fights you actually picked. You won't be penalised for fights you didn't pick — but you also won't get credit. Pick more fights for a more accurate reflection of your knowledge.
When are results entered after an event?
Results are usually entered within 24 hours of an event ending. Once entered, your picks update automatically — you'll see the ✓ and ✗ on your picks page and your stats will update.
How do groups work — can I be in more than one?
Currently each account is in one group at a time. Your picks still count on the global leaderboard regardless of group. If you want to run a separate league, you'd need a separate account for now — multi-group support is on the roadmap.
What does the commissioner do?
The commissioner (group creator) can: set a season start date (which zeros stats and starts a fresh competition from that point), remove members from the group, and share the invite link. That's it — picks and results are the same for everyone.
How does the season start date work?
When the commissioner sets a season start date, the group leaderboard only counts picks made after that date. It's a clean reset without deleting anyone's historical data. The global leaderboard always shows all-time stats separately.
Are all UFC events included automatically?
Yes. Every upcoming UFC card is added to the site and automatically counts toward the season — no setup needed from your group. Just show up and pick.
How do I get the Hot Hand badge?
Win more than 50% of your judged picks in 5 consecutive events. Your current streak is shown on your profile — 3+ events lights it up with 🔥.
