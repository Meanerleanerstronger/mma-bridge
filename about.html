About

MMA Bridge

MMA Bridge is built for UFC fans who take the sport seriously. Preview every upcoming card, make fight predictions, track your accuracy across the full season, and compete against friends in a private group — all in one place.

Upcoming Events
Every scheduled UFC card — full fight listings, event countdowns, hype ratings, and add-to-calendar links.
Fight Forecast
Pick winners and methods before every event locks. Track your accuracy over the season and climb through eight tiers — from Rookie to Legend.
Leaderboard & Groups
Private season-long leagues with your friends. Global community standings, head-to-head challenges, a shared group wall, and a season recap at the end.
P4P Rankings
Community pound-for-pound rankings. Cast your vote and see how the MMA Bridge fanbase ranks the best fighters in the world.
Fight Reviews
Write and read community reviews of past events and fights. Rate cards, share your takes, and see how others scored the night.
Lucas Bot
AI assistant built for MMA. Ask about fighter records, matchup history, event details, or get a breakdown before fight night.

Questions, bug reports, suggestions, or general feedback — reach out at contact@mmabridge.com. We read and respond to everything.
